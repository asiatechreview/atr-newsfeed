About ATR Bulletin

ATR Bulletin is a rolling news feed for people who want to keep up with technology across Asia.

We follow developments across markets, companies and sectors, bringing together reporting from publications around the world in one place. Each update gives you the essential news in a short summary, with a link to the original source when you want the full story.

From startup funding and company earnings to AI, chips, crypto and policy, the Bulletin is designed for a quick scan throughout the day. You can search for a company or topic, browse the latest updates, or follow the RSS feed in your preferred reader.

Part of Asia Tech Review

ATR Bulletin is part of Asia Tech Review, an independent publication founded by Jon Russell in 2014. The Bulletin keeps you current between editions of the ATR newsletter, where we take a closer look at stories shaping the region’s tech industry.

ATR’s editorial decisions are made independently by Jon Russell and Sai Narayan. Jon is also an executive director at Ellerton & Co, a communications firm. Ellerton does not commission, review or approve ATR’s coverage. If we cover a current Ellerton client, we disclose the relationship.
